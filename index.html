05 Why this, why Fold

  • Retention. A monthly roast is a reason to open the app that isn't a bill or a warning. People come back to see what they'll be called this time.
  • Shareability. Nobody screenshots a pie chart. People do forward a line about their Swiggy habit to the group chat, and every forward is a Fold ad.
  • Behaviour change. Every roast ends with one concrete fix and a rupee number. The joke gets attention; the fix is what sticks.

On my own inbox, 64% of what an SMS parser called spending was self-transfers and card bill payments. Fold's Account Aggregator data already solves that; the roast layer is what's missing.
